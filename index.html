Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 04/29/2025

App Name: SoleLife

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our app collects, uses, and protects your information when using the app.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information or user data on external servers. All data used by this app is stored locally on your device and is never shared with third parties.

2. Use of Apple HealthKit

Our app integrates with Apple’s HealthKit framework to access your workout data, specifically:

  • Workout type (e.g., running)
  • Distance run

This data is used solely to:

  • Track the number of miles you have run
  • Allow you to assign those miles to sneakers you have created in the app

We do not access any other HealthKit data beyond what is required for the features described above.

We do not use your HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or analytics.

HealthKit data remains on your device and is not transmitted or stored remotely.

3. Data Security

All data is stored securely on your device. We do not transmit, back up, or sync your data to any external server or cloud storage. You are in full control of your data.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share your data with any third parties. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.

5. User Consent

By enabling HealthKit access, you consent to our app accessing and using HealthKit data solely for the purposes outlined above.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app and at the URL where this policy is hosted.
